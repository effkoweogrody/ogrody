# Sceny do animacji

Wygenerowane wbudowanym narzędziem Imagegen.

## garden-evening

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: a shared website hero background for a premium Polish garden design studio, a single very wide cinematic 3:1 landscape photograph. Scene: an intimate lush designed garden at blue hour, one mature slender deciduous multi-stem tree clearly occupying the far left quarter, graceful branches and an olive-green canopy reaching across the top left. Meandering gravel path and soft perennial flower beds, cream hydrangeas and grasses. A small dark weathered steel circular fire bowl with a low real orange flame in the background on the right, at approximately 78 percent across and 62 percent down, fully visible and distinct against its dark surroundings. Calm natural garden rather than a house facade, faint warm garden lighting. Lower left and middle relatively simple dark foliage and lawn so white page titles remain readable. Composition wide and horizontal; tree trunk entirely on left, fire bowl on right, no foreground furniture obscuring either. Editorial photography, believable textures, detailed sharp foliage, natural exposure, refined deep greens, dusk blue, warm amber. This is a still source plate for adding subtle falling leaves and fire animation in code, so do not draw falling leaves, sparks, motion blur or artificial animation. No people, no words, no logos, no watermark, no UI, no collage. Render as large and sharp as available.

## papers-by-window

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: editorial still photograph for the image half of a 50/50 website section, portrait 4:5 composition. A small stack of loose warm ivory garden-design sheets on a pale oak desk beside a tall window on the left. One delicate pencil and watercolor planting plan on the top sheet, readable only as abstract botanical shapes and paths, absolutely no letters or words. Two casually overlapping cream sheets, natural paper edges and lightly raised corner, a single simple wooden pencil. Soft late afternoon sunlight enters from the left window with quiet shadows of window mullions and leafy branches falling across the paper; shadows are soft and sparse, preserving paper detail. Through the window a softly blurred garden. Camera at a gentle oblique angle, close editorial photograph, foreground mostly cream paper and warm light wood, airy space near upper left. Elegant natural textures, restrained sage and deep forest green accents, cream and warm neutral palette. Source plate for a gently moving leaf-shadow animation added in code. No hardcover notebook, no printed branding, no text, no people, no hands, no decorative clutter, no watermark, no collage, no interface. High detail and natural photographic realism.
## garden-evening-mobile

Wariant pionowy 4:5 wygenerowany wbudowanym Imagegen na podstawie szerokiej sceny. Zachowaj ten sam ogród o zmierzchu. Przekomponuj kadr zamiast przycinać: pień i korona drzewa przy lewej krawędzi, całe stalowe palenisko z niskim płomieniem około 79% szerokości i 72% wysokości. Ciemne, spokojne tło dla jasnego tytułu; ta sama roślinność, paleta i światło. Bez ludzi, tekstu, znaków wodnych, spadających liści i iskier — te efekty dodaje kod.
